# Thai learning site build log

## 3 August 2026

### Why I started

I wanted to improve my Thai.

I couldn't find a learning experience that matched how I naturally consume content. Rather than collecting vocabulary, I wanted to learn through songs and stories.

So I decided to build one.

---

### What I built

- First GitHub Pages site
- Interactive song lesson
- Phrase-by-phrase navigation
- Sticky video player
- Writing practice
- Interactive story prototype

---

### What surprised me

The biggest surprise wasn't building the website.

It was discovering that the interaction mattered more than the content.

Showing one phrase at a time felt dramatically easier than presenting a full page of lyrics.

---

### Product observations

**Songs**

Great motivation.

Harder to control vocabulary progression.

**Stories**

Much easier to scaffold.

Vocabulary can be introduced naturally.

Probably the strongest long-term format.

---

### Decisions

**Keep**

- One phrase at a time
- Previous / Next navigation
- Writing practice
- Real content
- Short explanations

**Avoid**

- Walls of text
- Long grammar lectures
- Vocabulary lists without context

---

### Questions

- Should users tap words for definitions?
- Should stories have illustrations?
- Should every lesson have audio?
- Should songs and stories use the same lesson engine?

---

### Rejected ideas

**Rejected:** Showing the full song lyrics by default.

**Reason:** People stop interacting and just scroll.

**Decision:** Reveal one phrase at a time instead.

---

### Things I learned

AI didn't just help me write code.

It helped me prototype an idea that would previously have stayed in my notebook.

Building a working product taught me more than planning one.

---
