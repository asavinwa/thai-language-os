The loop

Use Thai. Notice something. Feed it back into the system.

The diary is not another study task. It sits on top of the things I am already doing: family conversations, music, stories, Netflix, travel, speaking practice and ordinary daily life.

Not a streak

I’ll write when there is something worth learning from.

No forced daily posts. The useful rhythm is experience → reflection → next experiment.

Use Thai in real life. Keep the lesson. Try again.
